ISO/IEC 27001:2022 — INFORMATION SECURITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (ISMS) — ANNEX A SUMMARY

A.5 — Organizational Controls
Information security policies shall be approved by top management, reviewed annually, and communicated to all personnel and relevant external parties.

A.6 — People Controls
Background verification for candidates handling sensitive data. Security awareness training at onboarding and annually thereafter.

A.8 — Technological Controls
Asset inventory with ownership assignment. Vulnerability management with defined remediation SLAs: Critical 7 days, High 30 days.

A.8.24 — Cryptography
Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+) for customer PII and payment card data. Key rotation every 12 months.

A.5.29 — Business Continuity
RTO of 4 hours and RPO of 1 hour for critical banking systems. Annual BCP drill with documented lessons learned.

Certification audit cycle: Surveillance audit annually, recertification every 3 years by accredited body.
