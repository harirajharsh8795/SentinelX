SEBI — LISTING OBLIGATIONS AND DISCLOSURE REQUIREMENTS (LODR) — CYBERSECURITY & RESILIENCE

Regulation 27 — Risk Management Committee
Listed entities must constitute a Risk Management Committee with at least one independent director having cybersecurity expertise.

Regulation 47 — Cybersecurity and Cyber Resilience Framework
Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs) and stock brokers shall implement:
- Annual VAPT (Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing)
- 6-hour incident reporting to SEBI for critical cyber events
- Board-approved Cyber Crisis Management Plan (CCMP)

Regulation 30 — Material Event Disclosure
Cyber incidents affecting operations, customer data, or trading systems must be disclosed to stock exchanges within 24 hours.

Annexure — Data Localization
Critical securities market data shall be stored within India. Cross-border transfer requires prior SEBI approval.
