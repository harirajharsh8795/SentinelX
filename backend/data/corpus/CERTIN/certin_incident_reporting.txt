CERT-IN DIRECTIONS UNDER SECTION 70B, IT ACT 2000 — CYBERSECURITY INCIDENT REPORTING

Direction 1 — Mandatory Reporting Entities
All service providers, intermediaries, data centres, and government bodies must report cyber incidents to CERT-In within 6 hours of detection.

Direction 2 — Log Retention
Entities shall maintain logs of all ICT systems for 180 days rolling within Indian jurisdiction. Logs must include timestamps, source IP, destination IP, and user identity.

Direction 3 — Reportable Incidents
- Data breach affecting 1000+ records
- Ransomware attack on critical infrastructure
- DDoS causing 30+ minutes downtime
- Compromise of privileged accounts

Direction 4 — Point of Contact
Each entity must designate a CISO-level contact and register with CERT-In portal within 30 days.

Direction 5 — Non-Compliance
Failure to report within prescribed timeline may result in direction to cease operations under Section 70B.
