RESERVE BANK OF INDIA — MASTER DIRECTION ON KYC (KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER) NORMS

Section 1 — Applicability
All Scheduled Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks, and regulated entities shall implement risk-based KYC policies approved by the Board.

Section 2 — Customer Due Diligence
Banks must obtain PAN, proof of identity, and proof of address for all individual customers. Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) is mandatory for Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and high-risk jurisdictions.

Section 3 — Periodic Updation
Full KYC refresh shall be completed at least once every 10 years for low-risk customers and every 2 years for high-risk customers. Re-KYC triggers include change in ownership, suspicious activity, or address mismatch.

Section 4 — Reporting Obligations
Suspicious Transaction Reports (STR) must be filed with FIU-IND within 7 days of detection. Non-compliance attracts monetary penalty under Section 13 of PMLA.

Section 5 — Digital Onboarding
Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) is permitted subject to liveness detection, geo-tagging, and audit trail retention for 5 years.
